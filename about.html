Background and motivation

I am an MSc Artificial Intelligence student at the University of Essex. My academic portfolio documents how I develop and apply AI knowledge through programming, research, practical exercises and reflection.

I am particularly interested in responsible AI, data management and AI governance, including how organisations can adopt AI effectively while managing risks and protecting people.

Portfolio purpose

This website is a developing record of my learning rather than a claim that every topic has been mastered. I aim to show what I studied, how I approached problems, what I learned and what I would improve.
