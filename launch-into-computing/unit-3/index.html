Unit 3: Principles of Computer Science

Launch into Computing | University of Essex Online

1. Unit Overview

Unit 3 introduces the fundamental principles of computer science, focusing on algorithms, data structures and computational complexity.

The unit explores sorting and searching algorithms alongside arrays, linked lists and trees. It examines how computational complexity helps evaluate algorithm efficiency in enterprise computing systems.

2. Learning Outcomes

  1. Identify and analyse key principles of algorithms and data structures.
  2. Gather and synthesise information to apply computational principles to business challenges.
  3. Evaluate the efficiency and suitability of algorithms and data structures for specific computing problems.
