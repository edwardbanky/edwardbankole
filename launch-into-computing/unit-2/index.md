## 1. Unit Overview

This week focuses on the logical and mathematical principles underpinning computing, including Boolean logic, logic gates, and set theory. These concepts form the foundation of computational systems, programming languages, and algorithm design. Students will explore how Boolean algebra is used to design circuits and optimise computations, while set theory serves as a critical tool for structuring data and defining relationships in computing systems.

By engaging in hands-on activities and analysing real-world applications, students will connect theoretical knowledge with its practical implementation in areas like digital circuits, database design, and computational logic. The integration of these concepts into enterprise computing systems will also be discussed.

On the completion of this unit, you will be able to:
Identify and critically analyse the role of Boolean logic and set theory in solving computing challenges.
Gather and synthesise information on logic and mathematical applications in enterprise systems.
Evaluate tools and techniques (e.g., logic gates, truth tables) to solve computational challenges.

#Case Study Activity
Case Study: Boolean Logic in Search Engines and Digital Advertising (2022)

#Completion requirements
Search engines like Google and Bing rely heavily on Boolean logic to filter and rank search results. Boolean operators such as AND, OR, and NOT are used to optimize search queries, ensuring users get precise results. In digital advertising, Boolean logic plays a critical role in programmatic ad buying by matching customer preferences with advertising content.

For example, in 2022, Google reported advancements in its Boolean-based algorithms that improved search accuracy by 15%, particularly in regional and specific keyword searches. Similarly, advertising platforms like Meta (formerly Facebook) use Boolean expressions to refine audience targeting, ensuring that advertisements align with user preferences and behaviours.

This case study highlights the practical use of Boolean algebra in everyday technologies, showcasing its impact on user experience, marketing strategies, and business revenue.
