• Unit 2: ## Unit 2: Logical Foundations of Computing: Boolean Algebra, Gates, and Set Theory Welcome to Unit 2. This week focuses on the logical and mathematical principles underpinning computing, including Boolean logic, logic gates, and set theory. These concepts form the foundation of computational systems, programming languages, and algorithm design. Students will explore how Boolean algebra is used to design circuits and optimise computations, while set theory serves as a critical tool for structuring data and defining relationships in computing systems. By engaging in hands-on activities and analysing real-world applications, students will connect theoretical knowledge with its practical implementation in areas like digital circuits, database design, and computational logic. The integration of these concepts into enterprise computing systems will also be discussed. #### On the completion of this unit, you will be able to: - Identify and critically analyse the role of Boolean logic and set theory in solving computing challenges. - Gather and synthesise information on logic and mathematical applications in enterprise systems. - Evaluate tools and techniques (e.g., logic gates, truth tables) to solve computational challenges. * --- ### Reading The reading this week introduces set theory, logic, statistical probabilities and inference. 1.
