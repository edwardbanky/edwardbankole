# Unit 1: What is Computing?

**Module:** Launch into Computing  
**Programme:** MSc Artificial Intelligence  
**Institution:** University of Essex Online

## 1. Unit Overview

This unit introduces computing as a foundation for technological innovation, business transformation and artificial intelligence. It explores Software Engineering, Data Science, Cybersecurity, Information Management, Enterprise IT and Artificial Intelligence.

The unit also examines equality, diversity and inclusion (EDI), including the role of computing in improving accessibility and reducing barriers.

## 2. Learning Outcomes

By the end of this unit, the intended learning outcomes are to:

1. Identify and critically analyse computing challenges and processes in business systems.
2. Gather and synthesise information from multiple sources to understand computing concepts.
3. Articulate the legal, social, ethical and professional considerations in computing.
